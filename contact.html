Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer home delivery for products?
Yes, we offer home delivery across Chennai and nearby areas. Orders above ₹999 qualify for free delivery. For bulk orders, we provide same-day or next-day delivery as per availability. Contact us for delivery outside Chennai.
Do you provide installation services along with product purchase?
Absolutely! We offer professional installation services for all products we sell — fans, lights, MCBs, inverters, and more. Our certified electricians will install your products safely and efficiently. Contact us to schedule an installation visit.
What is your return and warranty policy?
We offer a 7-day return policy on all products in their original, unused condition with original packaging. Product warranties are as per the manufacturer's terms (typically 1–3 years). Bring your purchase receipt to the store for warranty claims or returns.
Do you cater to bulk or wholesale orders?
Yes, we welcome bulk orders from contractors, builders, electricians, and businesses. We offer special wholesale pricing for large quantities. Please contact our sales team at sales@brightelectricals.com or call +91 91234 56789 to discuss your requirements and get a custom quotation.
How do I book an electrical repair or installation service?
You can book a service by calling us, sending a WhatsApp message, or filling out the contact form on this page. Mention your requirement, address, and preferred date/time slot. Our team will confirm the booking within a few hours and send a certified electrician to your location.
Are your electricians certified and trained?
Yes, all our service electricians hold valid ISI (Indian Standards Institute) electrical contractor licenses and undergo regular skill-upgradation training. We also carry full liability insurance for all service work carried out at customer premises.
