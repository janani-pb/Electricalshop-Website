Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide service on weekends and holidays?
Yes! We offer services 7 days a week, including Sundays and public holidays. Emergency repair services are available 24/7. Just call us or WhatsApp your requirement.
Are your electricians certified?
All our electricians are Government-certified (Licensed Electrical Contractors) with minimum 5 years of experience. They undergo regular safety training and are fully insured.
What areas in Chennai do you cover?
We cover all of Chennai and surrounding areas including Adyar, Anna Nagar, T. Nagar, Velachery, OMR, Porur, Tambaram, and more. Contact us to confirm service availability in your area.
How long does a typical home wiring job take?
A standard 2BHK wiring job takes 2–3 days. A 3BHK or villa project takes 4–7 days depending on complexity. We provide a detailed timeline before starting the work.
Do you provide warranty on service work?
Yes, all our service work comes with a 30-day workmanship warranty. For new wiring installations, we provide a 1-year warranty. Products installed also carry their respective manufacturer warranties.
What is included in the AMC package?
Our AMC package includes 4 scheduled inspections per year, priority breakdown service with 4-hour response time, a full safety audit report, free minor repairs during inspections, and a 15% discount on spare parts and materials.
