About This Product

The Havells 18W LED Panel Light is an ideal lighting solution for modern interiors. Its ultra-slim design makes it easy to install on false ceilings, while the powerful LED driver ensures consistent brightness without any flicker. Uses high-quality SMD LEDs that provide uniform light distribution, eliminating hot spots and dark zones.
