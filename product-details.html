About This Product

The Havells 18W LED Panel Light is an ideal lighting solution for modern interiors. Its ultra-slim design makes it easy to install on false ceilings, while the powerful LED driver ensures consistent brightness without any flicker. The panel uses high-quality SMD LEDs that provide uniform light distribution across the entire panel surface, eliminating hot spots and dark zones.

This product is perfect for offices, shops, hospitals, hotels, homes, and any space where energy-efficient, aesthetically pleasing lighting is required. With a rated life of 50,000 hours, you won't need to replace it for over 11 years of regular use.
