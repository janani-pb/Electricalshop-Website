Our Story

Bright Electricals — Powering Chennai Since 2005

Founded in 2005 by Mr. K. Subramaniam, Bright Electricals started as a small neighbourhood electrical shop in Anna Salai, Chennai. With a commitment to stocking only genuine, quality-certified products and offering honest advice, the shop quickly earned the trust of residents, contractors, and businesses alike.

Over two decades, we have grown into one of Chennai's most reputed electrical product retailers, stocking over 1,200 products from 50+ brands including Havells, Crompton, Finolex, Polycab, Anchor, Schneider, and Legrand. Today, we serve over 5,000 happy customers and continue to expand our product range and services every year.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide every customer — homeowner, contractor, or business — with access to the highest quality electrical products at fair prices, backed by expert advice and reliable after-sales support. We believe that safe, efficient electrical systems start with genuine products and professional installation.

Our Vision

To become Tamil Nadu's most trusted electrical products and services brand — known for our uncompromising commitment to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction. We aim to empower communities with energy-efficient electrical solutions that contribute to a greener, safer future.

Safety First

We stock only ISI-certified, BEE-rated products. Your family's safety is non-negotiable.

Innovation

We continuously update our range with the latest energy-efficient and smart electrical solutions.

Reliability

Two decades of consistent service has made us the go-to electrical store for thousands.

Sustainability

We actively promote LED lighting, BLDC fans, and energy-saving products to reduce carbon footprint.
